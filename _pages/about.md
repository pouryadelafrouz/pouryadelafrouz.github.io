I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at <a href="https://www.foxchase.org/">Fox Chase Cancer Center</a>, working with <a href="https://comp-epi-lee-lab.org/">Dr. Hayan Lee</a>. I earned my Ph.D. in Biomedical Engineering from the <a href="https://www.uic.edu/">University of Illinois Chicago (UIC)</a>.


My research focuses on computational genomics, epigenomics, and the three-dimensional organization of genomes. I combine computational modeling, high-throughput sequencing, and statistical analysis to investigate genome structure, regulation, and their roles in biological processes and disease.


My current research interests include mitochondrial DNA organization and epigenetics, telomere biology, and nanopore sequencing. I am particularly interested in developing and applying computational approaches to better understand genome organization and its implications for aging and cancer.
